Chocolate Cake

A simple and delicious chocolate cake recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients.
  3. Add the eggs and milk.
  4. Mix everything together.
  5. Bake until ready.
